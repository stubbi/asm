HOW SPONSORSHIP WORKS

Clear terms. Independent work.

We agree the term, recognition, availability, and payment route before sponsorship starts.

Funding and evidence

Funding can support paid maintainers, infrastructure, security reviews, backups, and recovery tests.

For principal programmes, quarterly reports cover the budget, completed work, evidence, delays, and remaining risks.

Results and evidence remain public, including failed checks and inconclusive findings.

Agreements and renewal

Principal and Lead sponsorships use 12-month agreements, with annual or monthly payments. There is no standard annual discount.

Core, Company, and individual support are arranged monthly. Confirm renewal and cancellation terms before payment.

Recognition lasts for the paid term. Historical acknowledgements remain historical.

Recognition and capacity

Higher tiers receive more prominent recognition. Affiliated companies count together when applying the Principal and Lead limits.

We agree approved names, logos, links, and recognition consent before publication. Paid website links use sponsored-link attributes.

The badge states support for Asm. It does not imply that Asm endorses a sponsor's product.

Technical independence

Sponsorship does not change package rankings, publication rules, security checks, or technical conclusions.

It does not include industry-wide exclusivity, feature guarantees, consulting, or support response guarantees.

Any commissioned engineering or support needs a separate scope and agreement.
